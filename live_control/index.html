Connection & control limits

This page is served by the laptop connected to the selected Tello Wi-Fi. Keep other drone apps and flight runners closed. This bridge supports one drone and one browser operator.

Movement commands expire after 0.3 seconds without renewal. A stale camera, telemetry, browser heartbeat or worker deadline latches a landing request during an owned flight. A lost radio link may prevent delivery. Landing acknowledgment is not proof of touchdown; restart only after landing and review.

Manual control has no validated world-position geofence. Policy mode uses the repository policy and Maude's guards; real policy runs require verified mapping/calibration. RC is capped at 20. Camera age measures decode time, not sensor capture latency. No trained RL checkpoint is loaded.
